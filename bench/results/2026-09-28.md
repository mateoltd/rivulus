## discord.js side — MANUAL (why it cannot be done here)

`bench/djs-equiv/bot.js` calls `client.login(DISCORD_TOKEN)` and reads
`client.guilds.cache.size`: it needs a live token AND a bot present in
100–50,000 guilds for the matrix. No token may pass through this loop
(standing rule since 2026-09-27), and no such guild membership exists.
Node 24 is present on this box, but without token+guilds the comparison
is unmeasurable. Nightly owner-side step; S2 stays template on the djs side.
